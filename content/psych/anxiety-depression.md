:::bottom
- Screen adults for **depression** (PHQ-2 → PHQ-9) and adults <65 for **anxiety** (GAD-7).
- **Always ask about suicidal thoughts**; urgent evaluation if a plan or intent.
- First-line: **CBT and/or an SSRI or SNRI**; response takes 4–6 weeks.
- Continue **6–12 months after remission** (longer after recurrent episodes).
- Avoid **benzodiazepines** as first-line therapy.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Depression and anxiety share genetics, stress biology and neurotransmitter systems (serotonin, norepinephrine) — the same drugs treat both.
- Rule out **medical mimics**: hypothyroidism, anemia, sleep apnea, substances, medications (glucocorticoids, interferon, β-blockers rarely).

## 💊 Choosing a drug

| Situation | Option |
|---|---|
| Most patients | Sertraline, escitalopram |
| Pain, fatigue | Duloxetine, venlafaxine |
| Insomnia, poor appetite | Mirtazapine |
| Sexual side effects, smoking cessation | Bupropion (not for anxiety; lowers seizure threshold) |
| Older adults | Sertraline, escitalopram (≤10 mg); avoid TCAs and paroxetine |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- **Bipolar screen** before an antidepressant — monotherapy can trigger mania.
- SSRIs + triptans/tramadol/linezolid → watch for **serotonin syndrome**; SSRIs cause **hyponatremia** (SIADH) in older adults and raise bleeding risk with NSAIDs or anticoagulants.
- Citalopram >40 mg (>20 mg if >60 years) prolongs QT.
:::
