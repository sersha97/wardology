:::bottom
- **Medications for OUD (buprenorphine, methadone) cut overdose death ~50%.** Detox alone does not.
- Start buprenorphine in **moderate withdrawal** (COWS ≥8–12) or use low-dose induction — otherwise **precipitated withdrawal**.
- **Naltrexone** needs 7–10 days opioid-free.
- **Naloxone** for every patient with OUD or high-dose opioids.
:::

## 🧠 Pharmacology in 60 seconds

| Drug | Action | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Methadone | Full μ agonist | Opioid treatment programs; **QT prolongation**, interactions |
| **Buprenorphine** | **Partial** agonist, very high affinity | Ceiling on respiratory depression; any prescriber (no X-waiver since 2023) |
| Naltrexone | Antagonist | Oral or monthly IM; must be abstinent |
| Naloxone | Short-acting antagonist | Rescue; may need repeat doses with fentanyl |

## 🔎 Opioid withdrawal

Yawning, rhinorrhea, lacrimation, **mydriasis**, piloerection, diarrhea, cramps, anxiety, tachycardia — miserable but rarely dangerous. Clonidine helps autonomic symptoms only.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Hospitalization (endocarditis, abscess, overdose) is a key moment to start MOUD.
- Continue buprenorphine or methadone perioperatively; add analgesia rather than stopping.
- Pregnancy: buprenorphine or methadone (not withdrawal).
:::
