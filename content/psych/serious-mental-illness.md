:::bottom
- Ask about **past mania/hypomania** before any antidepressant — SSRIs alone can flip bipolar patients into mania.
- **One manic episode (≥7 days or hospitalization) = bipolar I.**
- **Lithium levels rise** with thiazides, ACE inhibitors/ARBs, NSAIDs, dehydration. Monitor creatinine, TSH, calcium.
- Second-generation antipsychotics → monitor **weight, glucose, lipids, BP**; olanzapine and clozapine are worst; aripiprazole/ziprasidone/lurasidone best.
:::

## 🔎 Mood episodes

| | Duration | Impairment | Psychosis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mania | ≥7 days | Marked | Possible |
| Hypomania | ≥4 days | None marked | No |
| Major depression | ≥2 weeks | Yes | Possible |

## 💊 Drug toxicities to know

| Drug | Problem |
|---|---|
| **Lithium** | Tremor, polyuria (**nephrogenic DI**), hypothyroidism, hyperparathyroidism, CKD; toxicity → ataxia, confusion |
| Valproate | Hepatotoxicity, pancreatitis, **teratogen** (neural tube), weight gain |
| Lamotrigine | **SJS/TEN** (titrate slowly) |
| Carbamazepine | SJS/TEN (HLA-B*15:02), hyponatremia, agranulocytosis |
| **Clozapine** | **Agranulocytosis** (ANC monitoring), myocarditis, seizures, constipation |
| Antipsychotics | **QT prolongation**, EPS, tardive dyskinesia, NMS, hyperprolactinemia |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Life expectancy in serious mental illness is 10–20 years shorter — mostly **cardiovascular**. Treat smoking (varenicline is safe), lipids, diabetes.
- Metformin reduces antipsychotic weight gain.
:::
