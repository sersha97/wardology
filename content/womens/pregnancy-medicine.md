:::bottom
- **Planning pregnancy on ACEi/ARB → switch to labetalol or nifedipine ER.** Treat chronic HTN to <140/90 (CHAP).
- **Low-dose aspirin 81 mg from 12–28 weeks** for high preeclampsia risk.
- **Preeclampsia with severe features** → antihypertensives, **magnesium**, **delivery ≥34 weeks**.
- Graves first trimester → **PTU**; then methimazole. Levothyroxine dose ↑ ~30% at conception.
- **GDM → OGTT 4–12 weeks postpartum**, then every 1–3 years.
:::

## 💊 Switch before conception

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| ACE inhibitors, ARBs, spironolactone | Labetalol, nifedipine ER, methyldopa |
| Warfarin (embryopathy weeks 6–12) | **LMWH** |
| Statins | Stop |
| Valproate, carbamazepine | Lamotrigine, levetiracetam |
| Methotrexate, mycophenolate | Azathioprine, hydroxychloroquine |
| Methimazole (1st trimester) | PTU |
| DOACs | LMWH |

## 🔎 Hypertensive disorders

| Disorder | Definition |
|---|---|
| Chronic HTN | Before 20 weeks |
| Gestational HTN | After 20 weeks, no proteinuria/severe features |
| Preeclampsia | After 20 weeks + proteinuria or end-organ signs |
| **HELLP** | Hemolysis, elevated liver enzymes, low platelets |
| Eclampsia | Seizures → magnesium |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Preeclampsia and GDM are **risk enhancers** for future CVD — screen BP, lipids, glucose for life.
- Physiologic changes: ↓ BP in 2nd trimester, ↑ GFR (creatinine ~0.5), respiratory alkalosis, dilutional anemia.
:::
