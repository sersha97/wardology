:::bottom
- **Amenorrhea**: pregnancy test first → TSH, prolactin, FSH → progestin challenge/estradiol as needed.
- **PCOS** = 2 of 3 (oligo-ovulation, hyperandrogenism, polycystic ovaries) after excluding mimics. Screen glucose, lipids, sleep apnea.
- **Menopausal hormone therapy**: best for vasomotor symptoms in women **<60 or <10 years from menopause**; add **progestogen if uterus intact**.
- Contraindications: breast/endometrial cancer, VTE, stroke, CAD, liver disease, unexplained bleeding.
:::

## 🔎 Secondary amenorrhea

| Cause | Clue | Lab |
|---|---|---|
| Pregnancy | — | hCG |
| **PCOS** | Hirsutism, obesity, acanthosis | Testosterone ↑, LH:FSH ↑ |
| Hypothalamic | Low weight, exercise, stress | Low FSH/LH, low estradiol |
| Hyperprolactinemia | Galactorrhea, drugs | Prolactin ↑ |
| Thyroid | Symptoms | TSH |
| Primary ovarian insufficiency (<40 y) | Hot flashes | **FSH ↑** (twice) → karyotype, FMR1 |
| Asherman syndrome | Prior D&C | Normal hormones |

## 💊 Nonhormonal hot flash options

Fezolinetant, paroxetine (low dose), venlafaxine/escitalopram, gabapentin, oxybutynin, CBT. Avoid black cohosh.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Transdermal estrogen → lower VTE risk than oral.
- PCOS: unopposed estrogen → endometrial hyperplasia; protect with OCPs or cyclic progestin.
- Vaginal estrogen for genitourinary syndrome is safe even in many women who cannot take systemic MHT.
:::
