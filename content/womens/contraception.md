:::bottom
- **LARC** (IUDs, implant) are the most effective reversible methods (<1% failure/year).
- **No estrogen** with: migraine **with aura**, age ≥35 + smoking ≥15/day, VTE or thrombophilia, BP ≥160/100, vascular disease, breast cancer, decompensated cirrhosis, <21 days postpartum.
- Progestin-only and copper IUD are safe in almost all of those settings.
- **Enzyme inducers** (rifampin, carbamazepine, phenytoin) reduce pill, patch, ring and implant efficacy → use an IUD or DMPA.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Estrogen raises hepatic clotting factors → **VTE** risk (also stroke in susceptible women).
- Progestins thicken cervical mucus and (in higher doses) suppress ovulation; the LNG-IUD acts mainly locally.

## 💊 Methods at a glance

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| Levonorgestrel IUD | Up to 8 years; lighter periods; treats heavy menstrual bleeding |
| Copper IUD | Up to 10–12 years; also **emergency contraception** within 5 days |
| Etonogestrel implant | 3 (to 5) years; irregular bleeding |
| DMPA injection | Every 13 weeks; **bone loss** (reversible), weight gain |
| Combined pill/patch/ring | Regulate cycles; contraindications above |
| Progestin-only pill | Norethindrone (strict timing) or drospirenone |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Emergency contraception: copper IUD (most effective) > ulipristal (up to 5 days) > levonorgestrel (best within 72 h; less effective with BMI >30).
- Lamotrigine levels **fall** with estrogen-containing contraception.
- Combined methods are fine in uncomplicated **migraine without aura** under 35.
:::
