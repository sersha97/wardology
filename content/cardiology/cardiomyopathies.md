:::bottom
- **Thick wall + low voltage + HFpEF in an older man** → think **ATTR amyloid**. First exclude AL (serum free light chains, SPEP/UPEP with immunofixation), then **technetium-PYP scan**.
- **Young adult, viral prodrome, chest pain, troponin up, clean coronaries** → myocarditis → **cardiac MRI**.
- **AV block or VT in someone <60** → think **sarcoid** (cardiac MRI / FDG-PET), Lyme or a laminopathy.
- **New cardiomyopathy + fast rhythm for weeks** → control the rate; EF often recovers (**tachycardia-mediated**).
- **Dilated cardiomyopathy without a cause** → screen first-degree relatives (echo + ECG; genetic testing).
:::

## 🗺️ Map of the muscle diseases

| Disease | Clue | Key test | Treatment pearl |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ATTR amyloid** | Older man, HFpEF, carpal tunnel (bilateral), spinal stenosis, biceps tendon rupture, low voltage with thick wall | Exclude AL → **Tc-PYP/DPD scan** (grade 2–3 uptake = diagnostic) | **Tafamidis** or acoramidis; avoid digoxin and nondihydropyridine CCBs |
| **AL amyloid** | Proteinuria, macroglossia, periorbital purpura, neuropathy | Free light chains, immunofixation, fat pad/organ biopsy | Hematology — plasma cell therapy, fast |
| **Viral myocarditis** | Viral illness → chest pain, arrhythmia, troponin rise | **Cardiac MRI** (Lake Louise: edema + late gadolinium, non-ischemic pattern) | Supportive care, GDMT; **no competitive sport for 3–6 months** |
| **ICI myocarditis** | Weeks after PD-1/CTLA-4 drug, often with myositis/myasthenia | Troponin, ECG, CMR | Stop drug, **high-dose methylprednisolone** |
| **Giant cell myocarditis** | Fulminant HF + VT/heart block in a young adult | Endomyocardial biopsy | Immunosuppression, mechanical support |
| **Cardiac sarcoid** | AV block or VT age <60, hilar adenopathy | **CMR + FDG-PET** | Steroids; ICD often |
| **ARVC** | Young athlete, LBBB-morphology VT, T inversion V1–V3, **epsilon wave** | CMR, Task Force criteria | Stop endurance sport, beta-blocker, ICD if high risk |
| **Chagas** | Born in rural Latin America, **RBBB + left anterior fascicular block**, apical aneurysm, VT | *T. cruzi* serology (2 tests) | GDMT, ICD/pacing as needed; benznidazole for younger patients |
| **Tachycardia-mediated** | Persistent AF/flutter/atrial tachycardia or PVC burden >10–15% | EF recovery after rate/rhythm control | Rate or rhythm control, ablation |
| **Alcohol** | Heavy use >10 years | Diagnosis of exclusion | Abstinence — EF can recover |
| **Familial DCM** | Relatives with HF or sudden death | Genetic testing; screen relatives | LMNA/FLNC variants → early ICD |

## 🔎 The amyloid order of operations

:::flow
Q: Suspect cardiac amyloid (thick wall, low voltage, HFpEF, carpal tunnel)?
Y: Serum free light chains + serum and urine immunofixation FIRST
N: Look for another cause
:::

- **Abnormal light chains** → AL until proven otherwise → biopsy, hematology now.
- **Normal light chains + PYP grade 2–3** → **ATTR**, no biopsy needed → TTR gene test (hereditary V122I is common in Black Americans).

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- PYP can be falsely positive in AL — that is why light chains come first.
- Amyloid patients tolerate **beta-blockers, ACE inhibitors and nondihydropyridine CCBs poorly**; digoxin binds amyloid fibrils (toxicity).
- Myocarditis athletes: **no competitive sports for 3–6 months**, then return only with normal EF, troponin, Holter and exercise test.
- Chagas is a leading cause of cardiomyopathy in Latin America — ask where your patient grew up.
:::
