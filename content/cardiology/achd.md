:::bottom
- **Fixed split S₂** + RV enlargement + incomplete RBBB → **secundum ASD**. Close it if the right heart is enlarged (and no severe pulmonary hypertension).
- **PFO + cryptogenic embolic stroke, age 18–60, no other cause** → **PFO closure** + antiplatelet beats medical therapy alone.
- **Young adult with HTN, weak delayed femoral pulses, arm > leg BP, rib notching** → **coarctation**.
- **Eisenmenger** (reversed shunt, cyanosis, clubbing, erythrocytosis) → **avoid pregnancy**; do not close the defect.
- Repaired tetralogy of Fallot → late **pulmonary regurgitation**, RV dilation and VT.
:::

## 👂 Listen and look

| Lesion | Exam | ECG / imaging |
|---|---|---|
| Secundum ASD | **Fixed split S₂**, pulmonic flow murmur | Incomplete RBBB, right axis |
| Primum ASD | Like ASD + mitral cleft regurgitation | **Left axis** deviation |
| Sinus venosus ASD | Like ASD | Often anomalous pulmonary venous return (needs TEE/CMR) |
| Small VSD | **Loud, harsh holosystolic murmur** (small hole, big noise) | Normal |
| Coarctation | Brachial–femoral delay, systolic murmur in back, BP arms > legs | Rib notching, "figure 3" sign; associated **bicuspid valve** |
| PDA | Continuous "machinery" murmur | |
| Eisenmenger | Cyanosis, clubbing, loud P₂, no shunt murmur | RVH |

## 🛠️ What to do

- **ASD with RV enlargement** → device or surgical closure.
- **Coarctation** → stent or surgery; lifelong BP and aorta surveillance (recoarctation, aneurysm, intracranial aneurysm).
- **Eisenmenger** → pulmonary vasodilators, iron repletion, phlebotomy only for hyperviscosity symptoms, **air filters on IV lines** (paradoxical emboli), avoid pregnancy and dehydration.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Bicuspid valve + coarctation → think **Turner syndrome** in a woman with short stature.
- Patients with ACHD should be followed at an **ACHD center**.
- PFO closure is **not** for migraine or for stroke with another clear cause (AF, large-artery disease) or older age with vascular risk factors.
:::
