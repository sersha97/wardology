:::bottom
- **Anthracyclines** (doxorubicin): **dose-dependent, mostly irreversible** cardiomyopathy. Baseline echo; risk rises with dose >250 mg/m², age, prior chest radiation, HTN, existing LV disease.
- **Trastuzumab** (HER2): **not dose-dependent, usually reversible**. Echo every 3 months; EF drop → hold, start GDMT, often resume when EF recovers.
- **Checkpoint inhibitors**: myocarditis is rare but **fatal in up to ~40%** → stop the drug, **high-dose methylprednisolone** now.
- **5-FU / capecitabine**: coronary **vasospasm** (chest pain during infusion).
- **Radiation**: years later → CAD, valve disease, constriction.
:::

## 💊 Drug → heart problem

| Drug | Problem | Pearl |
|---|---|---|
| Doxorubicin, epirubicin | Cardiomyopathy (type I, cell death) | Dexrazoxane, liposomal forms reduce risk |
| Trastuzumab, pertuzumab | LV dysfunction (type II, stunning) | Usually recovers; worse after anthracycline |
| Pembrolizumab, nivolumab, ipilimumab | Myocarditis ± myositis/myasthenia | Troponin first weeks of therapy |
| 5-FU, capecitabine | Vasospasm, ACS | Stop, CCB/nitrates; often avoid rechallenge |
| VEGF inhibitors (bevacizumab, sunitinib) | **Hypertension**, HF, arterial clots | Treat BP; continue cancer therapy if possible |
| Ibrutinib | **Atrial fibrillation**, bleeding | Anticoagulation choice is tricky (interactions) |
| Arsenic trioxide, many TKIs | **QT prolongation** | Keep K > 4, Mg > 2 |
| Cisplatin | Thrombosis, HTN, hypomagnesemia | |
| Cyclophosphamide (high dose) | Hemorrhagic myocarditis | Acute, within days |

## 🔎 Surveillance

- Baseline **echo with global longitudinal strain (GLS)** before anthracycline or HER2 therapy.
- **Cancer therapy–related cardiac dysfunction**: LVEF falls ≥10 points to <50%, or a relative GLS drop >15%.
- Trastuzumab: echo **every 3 months** during therapy.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Trastuzumab EF drop: hold, treat with ACEi/ARB + beta-blocker, **rechallenge after recovery** is common.
- Anthracycline damage can appear **years** later — lifelong awareness, treat risk factors.
- ICI myocarditis comes with **myositis and myasthenia-like weakness** ("triple M") — check CK and breathing.
:::
