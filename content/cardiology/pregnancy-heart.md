:::bottom
- **Normal pregnancy**: plasma volume +40–50%, cardiac output +30–50%, SVR falls. A **soft midsystolic flow murmur, S₃ and mild edema are normal**. Diastolic murmurs are never normal.
- **Peripartum cardiomyopathy**: EF <45% in the last month of pregnancy to 5 months postpartum, no other cause. GDMT after delivery (no ACEi/ARB/ARNI/MRA while pregnant). **Persistent EF <50% → advise against another pregnancy.**
- **Mechanical valve in pregnancy**: warfarin dose ≤5 mg/day → continue warfarin in 1st trimester is reasonable; higher dose → **dose-adjusted LMWH (anti-Xa peaks) or UFH** in weeks 6–12; switch to UFH before delivery. **No DOACs.**
- **Preeclampsia** doubles future CVD risk → lifelong annual BP and risk-factor checks.
- Highest-risk lesions (WHO class IV): **pulmonary arterial hypertension, Eisenmenger, severe symptomatic AS or MS, EF <30%, aorta >45 mm in Marfan** → pregnancy discouraged.
:::

## 💊 Drugs: yes and no

| Safe in pregnancy | Avoid in pregnancy |
|---|---|
| Labetalol, nifedipine, methyldopa | **ACE inhibitors, ARBs, ARNI, aldosterone antagonists** |
| Hydralazine + nitrates (HF) | **Atenolol** (growth restriction) |
| Metoprolol (HF, arrhythmia) | **Statins** (generally stop) |
| Furosemide (congestion) | **Warfarin >5 mg/day** in weeks 6–12, **DOACs**, amiodarone (thyroid) |
| UFH, LMWH, low-dose aspirin | SGLT2 inhibitors |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Peripartum cardiomyopathy: **bromocriptine** is considered (with anticoagulation) in severe cases — the prolactin cleavage product is cardiotoxic.
- Low-dose aspirin from **12 weeks** for women at high preeclampsia risk.
- Breastfeeding is compatible with enalapril, captopril, metoprolol and warfarin.
:::
