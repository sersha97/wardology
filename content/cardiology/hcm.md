:::bottom
- **Systolic murmur louder with Valsalva/standing, softer with squatting/handgrip** = HCM (or MVP).
- Diagnose with **echo** (wall ≥15 mm), **cardiac MRI** for risk; screen first-degree relatives.
- Obstruction symptoms → **beta-blocker or verapamil** → disopyramide or **mavacamten** → septal reduction.
- **Avoid** dehydration, nitrates, vasodilators and high-dose diuretics (they worsen obstruction).
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Sarcomere gene mutations (autosomal dominant) → asymmetric septal hypertrophy.
- In systole the mitral leaflet is pulled toward the septum (**systolic anterior motion**) → dynamic LVOT obstruction. **Smaller ventricle = more obstruction.**

## 🔎 Murmur maneuvers

| Maneuver | LV volume | HCM | Aortic stenosis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Valsalva, standing | ↓ | **Louder** | Softer |
| Squatting, leg raise | ↑ | Softer | Louder |
| Handgrip | ↑ afterload | Softer | Softer |

## ⚡ Sudden death risk → consider ICD

- Prior cardiac arrest or sustained VT (secondary prevention).
- Family history of HCM sudden death, unexplained syncope, wall ≥30 mm, LVEF <50%, apical aneurysm, extensive LGE on MRI.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Athlete's heart vs HCM: athlete's heart regresses with detraining, has a dilated LV and normal diastolic function.
- Pregnancy is usually tolerated; avoid hypovolemia.
- Atrial fibrillation in HCM → anticoagulate **regardless of CHA₂DS₂-VASc**.
:::
