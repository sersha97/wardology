:::bottom
- **Tension** (hypotension, hypoxemia, tracheal shift) → **needle decompression now**, no imaging first.
- **Primary spontaneous**, stable → **observation or needle aspiration / small-bore drain**; stop smoking.
- **Secondary** (COPD, CF, ILD) → usually **admit and drain** — less reserve.
- Surgery (VATS) for recurrence, persistent air leak, bilateral, or pilots/divers.
:::

## 🔎 Types

| Type | Patient | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Primary spontaneous | Tall, thin, young male smoker | Conservative if stable |
| Secondary spontaneous | Underlying lung disease, older | Drain, admit; consider pleurodesis |
| Iatrogenic | After central line, biopsy, ventilation | Often observe; drain if large or ventilated |
| **Tension** | Any; ventilated patients | Decompress, then chest tube |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Catamenial pneumothorax → thoracic endometriosis.
- HIV with PJP, LAM (young women, cysts), Birt-Hogg-Dubé — cystic lung disease causes.
- No flying until resolved; no diving unless surgically treated.
:::
