:::bottom
- **IPF** = older ex-smoker, Velcro crackles, clubbing, **UIP on HRCT** (basal, subpleural honeycombing) → **antifibrotic** (nintedanib, pirfenidone). **No steroids.**
- **HP**: exposure (birds, mold, hot tub) + centrilobular nodules + **air trapping** → **remove antigen**.
- **SSc** (anti–Scl-70) → screen with **HRCT + PFTs** at diagnosis.
- **Löfgren** (EN + hilar nodes + ankle arthritis + fever) → NSAIDs, no biopsy.
:::

## 🔎 HRCT patterns

| Pattern | Where | Think |
|---|---|---|
| **UIP** | Basal, subpleural, honeycombing | IPF, RA-ILD, asbestosis |
| **NSIP** | Basal ground glass, subpleural sparing | **SSc**, myositis, drugs |
| **Centrilobular nodules + mosaic air trapping** | Upper/mid | **Hypersensitivity pneumonitis** |
| **Perilymphatic nodules + hilar nodes** | Upper | **Sarcoidosis** |
| Organizing pneumonia | Peripheral consolidations | Cryptogenic, drugs, CTD → steroids |

## 🧠 Sarcoidosis in 60 seconds

- Noncaseating granulomas; Black women; hilar nodes, uveitis, skin (erythema nodosum, lupus pernio), **hypercalcemia** (granuloma calcitriol), cardiac (heart block, VT), CNS.
- Treat **organ-threatening** disease with prednisone → methotrexate → TNF inhibitors.
- Screen all patients: **ECG, eye exam, calcium, creatinine, LFTs**.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Drugs causing ILD: **amiodarone, nitrofurantoin, methotrexate, bleomycin**, checkpoint inhibitors.
- Restrictive PFTs with **low DLCO** = parenchymal disease; **normal DLCO** = chest wall/neuromuscular.
:::
