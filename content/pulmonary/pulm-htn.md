:::bottom
- **Mean PA pressure >20 mm Hg** on **right heart catheterization** = PH.
- Look for the cause: **left heart (group 2)** and **lung disease/hypoxia (group 3)** are most common.
- **V/Q scan** to screen for **CTEPH** (group 4) — curable by endarterectomy.
- **Group 1 PAH** → specialist center; vasoreactive → CCB; others → ERA + PDE-5i ± prostacyclin, sotatercept.
:::

## 🔎 WHO groups

| Group | Cause |
|---|---|
| 1 PAH | Idiopathic, heritable (BMPR2), **drugs (fenfluramine, methamphetamine)**, CTD (**limited SSc**), HIV, portal hypertension, congenital shunts |
| 2 | **Left heart disease** (HFpEF, valves) |
| 3 | COPD, ILD, **OSA**, hypoxia |
| 4 | **CTEPH** |
| 5 | Sarcoidosis, myeloproliferative disorders, CKD, others |

## 🧠 Hemodynamics in 60 seconds

- **Pre-capillary** (groups 1, 3, 4): PAWP ≤15, PVR >2 Wood units.
- **Post-capillary** (group 2): PAWP >15.
- PAH-specific vasodilators can **worsen** group 2 PH (pulmonary edema).

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Exam: loud P₂, RV heave, TR murmur louder with inspiration, edema.
- Annual PAH screening in **systemic sclerosis** (echo, PFTs with DLCO, NT-proBNP).
:::
