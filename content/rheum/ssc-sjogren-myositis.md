:::bottom
- **Scleroderma renal crisis** (malignant HTN + AKI ± TMA, early diffuse SSc, steroids, anti–RNA pol III) → **captopril**, keep going even on dialysis.
- **SSc-ILD** (anti–Scl-70, diffuse) → screen all with **HRCT + PFTs/DLCO**; PAH screen yearly (limited SSc).
- **Sjögren** → anti-Ro/La; **MALT lymphoma** risk ↑ (parotid swelling, purpura, low C4, M protein).
- **Dermatomyositis** → **cancer screening** (CT chest/abdomen/pelvis ± pelvic US, CA-125 in high risk).
:::

## 🔎 Systemic sclerosis

| | Limited (CREST) | Diffuse |
|---|---|---|
| Skin | Distal to elbows/knees, face | Proximal, trunk |
| Antibody | **Anti-centromere** | **Anti–Scl-70**, anti–RNA pol III |
| Organ risk | **PAH**, calcinosis, telangiectasia | **ILD**, **renal crisis**, heart |

## 🔎 Inflammatory myopathies

| Disease | Clue |
|---|---|
| **Dermatomyositis** | Heliotrope, **Gottron papules**, shawl sign; cancer |
| Polymyositis / antisynthetase | **Anti–Jo-1**: ILD, mechanic's hands, arthritis, Raynaud |
| Immune-mediated necrotizing | **Statin** exposure, anti-HMGCR, very high CK |
| Inclusion body myositis | Older man, **finger flexor and quadriceps** weakness, steroid-resistant |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Raynaud phenomenon with digital ulcers → CCB, PDE-5 inhibitor, iloprost.
- Sjögren: also renal tubular acidosis (type 1), neuropathy, ILD, congenital heart block in offspring.
:::
