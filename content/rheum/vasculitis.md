:::bottom
- **GPA**: sinus + lung nodules/cavities + GN → **c-ANCA/PR3**.
- **MPA**: lung hemorrhage + GN, no ENT → **p-ANCA/MPO**.
- **EGPA**: **asthma + eosinophilia** + neuropathy → p-ANCA (~40%).
- Organ-threatening ANCA vasculitis → **steroids + rituximab** (or cyclophosphamide); avacopan spares steroids.
:::

## 🔎 By vessel size

| Size | Disease | Clue |
|---|---|---|
| Large | GCA, Takayasu | Age >50 headache/jaw claudication; young woman with arm claudication, pulse/BP difference |
| Medium | **PAN** | **Hepatitis B**, mononeuritis multiplex, renal **microaneurysms**, testicular pain; **spares lungs** |
| Medium | Kawasaki | Children |
| Small (ANCA) | GPA, MPA, EGPA | See above |
| Small (immune complex) | **IgA vasculitis** | Palpable purpura legs, arthralgia, abdominal pain, IgA nephropathy |
| Small (immune complex) | **Cryoglobulinemic** | **HCV**, purpura, low C4, RF+, GN |
| Small | Anti-GBM | Lung hemorrhage + GN |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Mononeuritis multiplex (foot drop + wrist drop) = vasculitis until proven otherwise.
- Drug-induced ANCA vasculitis: **levamisole-adulterated cocaine**, hydralazine, propylthiouracil.
:::
