:::bottom
- **ANA** is sensitive (screen); **anti-dsDNA and anti-Smith** are specific. **Low C3/C4 + high dsDNA** = active disease.
- **Hydroxychloroquine for everyone** with SLE (≤5 mg/kg/d; annual eye exams after 5 years).
- Proteinuria **≥0.5 g/day** → **kidney biopsy**.
- **APS with thrombosis → warfarin INR 2–3**. Avoid DOACs (especially triple-positive or arterial).
:::

## 🔎 Antibodies

| Antibody | Association |
|---|---|
| ANA | Screen (>95% sensitive) |
| Anti-dsDNA | **Nephritis**, tracks activity |
| Anti-Smith | Most specific |
| Anti-Ro/SSA | Subacute cutaneous lupus, **neonatal heart block**, Sjögren |
| **Anti-histone** | **Drug-induced lupus** (hydralazine, procainamide, isoniazid, TNF inhibitors) |
| Antiphospholipid | Clots, pregnancy loss |

## 🧠 APS in 60 seconds

- Criteria: thrombosis or pregnancy morbidity + **persistently positive** lupus anticoagulant, anticardiolipin or anti-β₂GPI (≥12 weeks apart).
- Lupus anticoagulant prolongs the aPTT **in vitro** but causes **clotting**.
- Pregnancy: LMWH + aspirin.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- SLE patients have high **premature cardiovascular** risk — manage risk factors aggressively.
- Fever in SLE on immunosuppression = infection until proven otherwise.
- Lupus pregnancy: plan during remission; continue hydroxychloroquine; screen anti-Ro.
:::
