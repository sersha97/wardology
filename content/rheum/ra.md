:::bottom
- **Symmetric polyarthritis** of MCPs, PIPs, wrists and MTPs, **morning stiffness >1 hour**, spares DIPs.
- **Anti-CCP** is the most specific test (~95%) and predicts erosions.
- **Start methotrexate early** (with folic acid); short glucocorticoid bridge if needed.
- **Treat to target**: reassess every 1–3 months; add a biologic or JAK inhibitor if not at target by 3–6 months.
- Screen for **hepatitis B/C and TB** before biologics; vaccinate early.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Autoimmunity to citrullinated proteins (smoking promotes citrullination in the lung) → inflamed **synovium (pannus)** erodes cartilage and bone.
- Cytokines **TNF, IL-6** drive both joint damage and systemic effects — fatigue, anemia, and **accelerated atherosclerosis**.

## 🔎 Diagnosis

- Labs: RF, anti-CCP, ESR/CRP, CBC, liver and kidney function.
- Hand and foot radiographs: early periarticular osteopenia; later **marginal erosions** and joint-space narrowing.

## 💊 Management

| Drug | Key monitoring / warnings |
|---|---|
| Methotrexate | CBC, liver tests, creatinine; teratogen; limit alcohol; pneumonitis |
| Hydroxychloroquine | Retinal exam (baseline, then yearly after 5 years) |
| Sulfasalazine, leflunomide | Cytopenias, liver; leflunomide teratogenic (washout) |
| TNF inhibitors | **Latent TB**, hepatitis B reactivation, avoid in HFrEF (NYHA III–IV) and demyelinating disease |
| JAK inhibitors | VTE, MACE, malignancy, zoster |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- New neck pain or hand paresthesias in long-standing RA → **atlantoaxial subluxation** — flexion-extension films before intubation.
- RA + splenomegaly + neutropenia = **Felty syndrome**.
- Leading cause of death in RA is **cardiovascular disease**.
:::
