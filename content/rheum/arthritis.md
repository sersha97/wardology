:::bottom
- **Hot swollen joint → aspirate** (cell count, Gram stain, culture, crystals) before antibiotics or steroids. Crystals don't exclude infection.
- **CPPD**: rhomboid, **weakly positive** birefringence, chondrocalcinosis (knee, wrist). Gout: needles, strongly negative.
- **Inflammatory back pain** (young, morning stiffness, better with exercise) → **NSAIDs + exercise** → TNF/IL-17 inhibitor.
- **Knee OA** → exercise, weight loss, **topical NSAIDs**. No glucosamine, hyaluronic acid or arthroscopy.
:::

## 🔎 Synovial fluid

| | Normal/OA | Inflammatory | Septic |
|---|---|---|---|
| WBC/µL | <2,000 | 2,000–50,000 | **>50,000** (often >100k) |
| Neutrophils | <25% | >50% | >90% |

## 🔎 Spondyloarthritis family

| Disease | Clues |
|---|---|
| Axial SpA / AS | Sacroiliitis, uveitis, HLA-B27 |
| **Psoriatic** | **DIP**, **dactylitis**, nail pitting, "pencil-in-cup" |
| Reactive | After GI (*Campylobacter*, *Salmonella*, *Shigella*) or GU (chlamydia) infection; conjunctivitis, urethritis |
| IBD-associated | Peripheral arthritis follows bowel activity |

## 🧠 Fibromyalgia in 60 seconds

Widespread pain + fatigue + sleep and cognitive symptoms, normal labs → **education, graded aerobic exercise, CBT** → duloxetine, pregabalin or low-dose amitriptyline. **No opioids.**

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- CPPD in patients <60 → check **ferritin/iron, calcium/PTH, magnesium, phosphate** (hemochromatosis, hyperparathyroidism, hypomagnesemia, hypophosphatasia).
- Gonococcal arthritis: young, tenosynovitis, pustules → ceftriaxone.
:::
