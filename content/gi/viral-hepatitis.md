:::bottom
- Screen **all adults once** for HCV (18–79) and HBV (HBsAg, anti-HBc, anti-HBs).
- **Anti-HBc+ (even HBsAg−) before rituximab** → entecavir or tenofovir prophylaxis.
- **HCV RNA+ → treat everyone**: sofosbuvir-velpatasvir 12 weeks or glecaprevir-pibrentasvir 8 weeks; check HBV first (DAAs can reactivate HBV).
- Vaccinate against HAV and HBV if not immune.
:::

## 🔎 Hepatitis B serologies

| HBsAg | Anti-HBc | Anti-HBs | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| − | − | + | Vaccinated |
| − | + | + | Resolved infection |
| + | IgM + | − | Acute |
| + | IgG + | − | Chronic |
| − | + | − | Resolved (waning anti-HBs), window period, or occult |

## 🧠 Who gets HBV treatment

- Cirrhosis with detectable DNA; ALT elevated + high DNA; pregnancy with high DNA (third trimester tenofovir); immunosuppression (prophylaxis); acute liver failure.
- Drugs: **entecavir, tenofovir** (high barrier to resistance).

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- HCC surveillance in HBV **without cirrhosis** for high-risk groups (Asian men >40, women >50, African ancestry, family history).
- Extrahepatic HCV: cryoglobulinemia, membranoproliferative GN, porphyria cutanea tarda, lichen planus.
- HBV: polyarteritis nodosa, membranous nephropathy.
:::
