:::bottom
- **Acute severe UC** (≥6 bloody stools + systemic toxicity) → **IV steroids**, exclude C. diff/CMV, **VTE prophylaxis**; day 3 no response → **infliximab or cyclosporine**.
- Avoid antidiarrheals and opioids in severe colitis (toxic megacolon).
- **Ileal resection/disease** → B₁₂ deficiency, bile acid diarrhea, **calcium oxalate stones**, gallstones.
- Before biologics: TB, hepatitis B, vaccines.
:::

## 🔎 Crohn vs UC

| | Crohn disease | Ulcerative colitis |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Mouth to anus, **terminal ileum**, skip lesions | **Rectum** upward, continuous |
| Depth | Transmural → **fistulas, strictures, abscesses** | Mucosal |
| Smoking | Worsens | "Protective" |
| Extraintestinal | Both: arthritis, uveitis, erythema nodosum, pyoderma | **PSC** more with UC |

## 🧠 Why ileal disease causes kidney stones

Lost bile acids → fat malabsorption → fatty acids bind calcium → **free oxalate** absorbed in colon → hyperoxaluria → **calcium oxalate stones**. Treat with low-fat, low-oxalate diet and calcium **with meals**.

## 💊 Treatment ladder

- Mild UC: **5-ASA** (oral + rectal). 5-ASA does little for Crohn.
- Moderate–severe: steroids for induction only → **biologics** (anti-TNF, vedolizumab, ustekinumab, IL-23 agents) or JAK inhibitors/S1P modulators.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- **Colon cancer surveillance colonoscopy** starts 8 years after onset of colitis (at diagnosis of PSC).
- Toxic megacolon: colon >6 cm + toxicity → surgery consult.
:::
