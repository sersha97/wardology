:::bottom
- **MASLD** → **FIB-4**: <1.3 low risk; 1.3–2.67 → **elastography**; >2.67 → hepatology.
- **Transferrin saturation >45%** → **HFE testing** (C282Y). Ferritin >1000 → assess fibrosis.
- **Cholestasis + AMA+** = PBC → **ursodiol**.
- **Acetaminophen** above nomogram line → **NAC now**; don't wait for ALT.
:::

## 🔎 Pattern recognition

| Pattern | Clue | Diagnosis |
|---|---|---|
| Hepatocellular, ALT > AST, metabolic syndrome | Echogenic liver | **MASLD** |
| AST:ALT >2, GGT ↑ | Alcohol | Alcohol-associated liver disease |
| ALT >1000 | Ischemia, drug (acetaminophen), acute viral | Acute injury |
| Young woman, high IgG, ASMA/ANA | Other autoimmune disease | **Autoimmune hepatitis** → steroids ± azathioprine |
| Cholestatic, AMA+ | Middle-aged woman, itch | **PBC** |
| Cholestatic, beaded ducts | UC, young man | **PSC** |
| Age <40, neuropsychiatric, hemolysis, low ceruloplasmin | Kayser-Fleischer rings | **Wilson disease** |
| Arthropathy (2nd/3rd MCP), diabetes, bronze skin | Transferrin sat ↑ | **Hemochromatosis** → phlebotomy |
| Emphysema + liver disease | Low AAT | **Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency** |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Statins are **safe** in MASLD — keep them.
- King's College criteria (acetaminophen ALF): pH <7.3, or INR >6.5 + creatinine >3.4 + grade III–IV encephalopathy → transplant listing.
- Second-line for PBC: elafibranor or seladelpar.
:::
