:::bottom
- **C. difficile, first episode → fidaxomicin** (or oral vancomycin). Fulminant → oral vancomycin 500 mg QID + IV metronidazole. ≥2 recurrences → fecal microbiota therapy.
- **Celiac**: tTG-IgA **+ total IgA**, while **eating gluten** → duodenal biopsy.
- **IBS**: Rome IV + no alarm features → CBC, CRP, fecal calprotectin, celiac serology. That's it.
- Alarm features: age ≥45, bleeding, weight loss, nocturnal symptoms, anemia, family history of CRC/IBD/celiac.
:::

## 🔎 Chronic diarrhea — sort by stool type

| Type | Clues | Think |
|---|---|---|
| Watery, secretory (no change with fasting) | Large volume, nocturnal | Microscopic colitis, bile acid, VIPoma, laxatives |
| Watery, osmotic (stops with fasting) | Stool osmotic gap >125 | Lactose, sorbitol, magnesium |
| Fatty | Greasy, weight loss | **Celiac**, pancreatic insufficiency, SIBO |
| Inflammatory | Blood, fever, calprotectin ↑ | **IBD**, infection, ischemia |

## 🧠 Microscopic colitis in 60 seconds

Older woman, watery diarrhea, normal colonoscopy → **random colon biopsies**. Triggers: **NSAIDs, PPIs, SSRIs**. Treat with **budesonide**.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Test only **unformed** stool for C. difficile; no test of cure.
- Dermatitis herpetiformis = celiac disease of the skin.
- Celiac + iron deficiency not responding to oral iron is a classic stem.
:::
