:::bottom
- **Psoriasis** >10% BSA or psoriatic arthritis → **biologic** (TNF, IL-17, IL-23). Screen metabolic and CV risk. Avoid systemic steroids (flare on taper).
- **Erythema nodosum** → ASO, chest radiograph (sarcoid), drug review (OCPs), IBD symptoms.
- **Scaly annular rash with central clearing** → **KOH** first; never topical steroids alone.
:::

## 🔎 Skin clues to systemic disease

| Skin finding | Think |
|---|---|
| **Acanthosis nigricans** | Insulin resistance; sudden/extensive → gastric adenocarcinoma |
| **Erythema nodosum** | Strep, sarcoid, IBD, OCPs, TB, pregnancy |
| **Pyoderma gangrenosum** | IBD, RA, myeloproliferative disease |
| Necrobiosis lipoidica | Diabetes |
| **Porphyria cutanea tarda** | **HCV**, alcohol, iron overload |
| **Dermatitis herpetiformis** | Celiac disease |
| Palpable purpura | Small-vessel vasculitis |
| Sweet syndrome (painful plaques + fever) | AML, drugs, infection |
| Seborrheic keratoses erupting suddenly (Leser-Trélat) | GI adenocarcinoma |

## 🔎 Groin rash triage

| | Tinea | Candida | Erythrasma |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scrotum | Spared | Involved, satellites | Variable |
| Test | KOH hyphae | KOH yeast | **Wood lamp coral-red** |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Lithium, beta-blockers, antimalarials and steroid withdrawal can trigger psoriasis.
- Onychomycosis: confirm (KOH, PAS, culture) before oral terbinafine.
:::
