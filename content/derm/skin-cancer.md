:::bottom
- **ABCDE + ugly duckling + evolution** → biopsy.
- Suspected melanoma → **full-thickness excisional biopsy, 1–3 mm margins** (or deep saucerization).
- **Breslow thickness** drives wide-excision margins and sentinel node biopsy.
- **Basal cell carcinoma**: pearly papule, telangiectasias — locally destructive, rarely metastasizes.
- **Squamous cell carcinoma**: scaly, crusted, sun-exposed — can metastasize, especially in transplant recipients.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- UV light causes DNA damage (signature C→T mutations). **Intermittent intense** sun (blistering burns) links most to melanoma and BCC; **cumulative** sun to SCC.
- **Actinic keratoses** are precancerous scaly lesions that can become SCC.

## 🔎 Diagnosis

| Lesion | Look | Biopsy |
|---|---|---|
| Melanoma | Asymmetric, irregular border, varied color, >6 mm, changing | Excisional |
| BCC | Pearly, rolled border, telangiectasias, central ulcer | Shave or punch |
| SCC | Hyperkeratotic, crusted nodule or plaque on sun-exposed skin | Shave or punch to dermis |
| Seborrheic keratosis | "Stuck-on," waxy, horn cysts | None unless atypical |

## 💊 Management

- Melanoma margins: in situ 0.5–1 cm; ≤1 mm 1 cm; 1–2 mm 1–2 cm; >2 mm 2 cm.
- BCC/SCC: excision; **Mohs surgery** for face, high-risk or recurrent tumors.
- Actinic keratoses: cryotherapy, topical 5-fluorouracil or imiquimod.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Sudden eruption of many seborrheic keratoses (**Leser-Trélat sign**) → look for GI adenocarcinoma.
- Organ transplant recipients have up to 65× the SCC risk → yearly skin examinations.
- Amelanotic and nodular melanomas break the ABCD rules — "**EFG**": elevated, firm, growing.
:::
