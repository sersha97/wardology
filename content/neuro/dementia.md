:::bottom
- **MCI** = objective decline in ≥1 domain **with preserved independence** in daily activities. **Dementia** = decline that **impairs independence**.
- Everyone with cognitive decline: **B12, TSH, CBC, CMP (glucose, calcium, sodium), depression screen, medication review** (anticholinergics, benzodiazepines, opioids), and **brain imaging (CT or MRI)** once.
- **Lewy body**: fluctuations, **visual hallucinations**, REM sleep behavior disorder, parkinsonism → **severe antipsychotic sensitivity**.
- **NPH**: gait first ("magnetic"), then urinary urgency, then cognition; ventriculomegaly → **high-volume LP tap test** → shunt.
- **Weeks–months decline + myoclonus** → **CJD**: MRI DWI (cortical ribboning), **CSF RT-QuIC**.
:::

## 🗺️ Which dementia?

| Type | Early clue | Imaging / tests | Treatment pearl |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alzheimer disease** | **Episodic memory** loss (repeats questions, misplaces items) | Hippocampal atrophy; amyloid PET, CSF Aβ42/tau, **plasma p-tau217** | Cholinesterase inhibitor, memantine; **anti-amyloid antibody** in early disease |
| **Lewy body** | Fluctuating attention, **well-formed visual hallucinations**, RBD, parkinsonism within a year of dementia | DaT scan (↓ striatal uptake) | Cholinesterase inhibitor (works well); **avoid haloperidol** |
| **Frontotemporal (behavioral)** | **Personality change, disinhibition, apathy**, compulsions, hyperorality; age 50s–60s; memory relatively spared | Frontal/temporal atrophy | SSRIs for behavior; cholinesterase inhibitors don't help |
| **Vascular** | Stepwise decline, executive slowing, focal signs | White matter disease, infarcts | Vascular risk control |
| **NPH** | **Wet, wobbly, wacky** — gait first | Ventriculomegaly out of proportion to atrophy | **Tap test → VP shunt** |
| **CJD** | Rapid decline over weeks, **startle myoclonus**, ataxia | DWI cortical ribboning, periodic sharp waves on EEG, **RT-QuIC** | Supportive; prion precautions |

## 💊 Anti-amyloid antibodies (lecanemab, donanemab)

- **Who**: MCI or mild dementia due to Alzheimer, with **confirmed amyloid** (PET or CSF).
- **Before**: MRI (exclude >4 microhemorrhages, superficial siderosis, prior macrohemorrhage), **APOE genotyping** (ε4 homozygotes have the highest **ARIA** risk), no anticoagulants.
- **ARIA**: amyloid-related imaging abnormalities — edema (ARIA-E) or hemorrhage (ARIA-H); surveillance MRIs during the first months.
- Benefit: modest slowing (about 25%–35%) of decline over 18 months.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Delirium = **acute, fluctuating inattention**; dementia = chronic. Dementia is the biggest risk factor for delirium.
- Depression can mimic dementia ("pseudodementia") — patients complain loudly about memory; people with dementia often deny it.
- Recreational **nitrous oxide** inactivates B12 → subacute combined degeneration even with a normal B12 level (check methylmalonic acid, homocysteine).
- Driving, firearms, finances and advance care planning are part of every dementia visit.
:::
