:::bottom
- **ALS**: **UMN + LMN signs together** (brisk reflexes with fasciculations and atrophy), **no sensory loss**, spared eye movements and sphincters → EMG; **riluzole**, multidisciplinary clinic, NIV for respiratory failure.
- **Lambert-Eaton**: proximal leg weakness that **improves with repeated effort**, **dry mouth/autonomic**, low reflexes → P/Q-type VGCC antibodies → **look for small cell lung cancer**.
- **CIDP**: GBS-like but **progresses >8 weeks**; demyelinating NCS, high CSF protein → **IVIG, steroids or PLEX** (steroids help CIDP but not GBS).
- **Painful diabetic neuropathy**: **duloxetine, pregabalin/gabapentin, TCAs, venlafaxine**; avoid opioids. Glucose control prevents, doesn't reverse.
- **Mononeuritis multiplex** (stepwise painful nerve deficits) → think **vasculitis**, diabetes, HIV, hepatitis C/cryoglobulins.
:::

## 🗺️ Where is the lesion?

| Level | Clues |
|---|---|
| **UMN** (brain/cord) | Spasticity, hyperreflexia, Babinski |
| **Anterior horn (ALS)** | UMN + LMN, fasciculations, no sensory |
| **Nerve root / plexus** | Dermatomal pain, myotomal weakness |
| **Peripheral nerve** | Distal, stocking-glove sensory, ↓ reflexes |
| **Neuromuscular junction** | Fatigable, ptosis/diplopia (MG); autonomic + ↑ with effort (LEMS) |
| **Muscle** | Proximal, symmetric, normal sensation and reflexes, ↑ CK |

## 🔎 Polyneuropathy first labs

**Glucose/HbA₁c (± 2-h OGTT), B12 (± MMA), SPEP with immunofixation, TSH, creatinine**; then by context: HIV, hepatitis C, alcohol, chemotherapy (platinum, taxanes, vinca), B6 excess.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- **Nitrous oxide** and long-term **metformin** cause B12 deficiency; B12 deficiency hits **dorsal columns + corticospinal tracts** (subacute combined degeneration).
- **ICU-acquired weakness**: symmetric flaccid weakness after sepsis/steroids/paralytics — critical illness polyneuropathy/myopathy.
- **Steroid myopathy**: proximal weakness, **normal CK**.
:::
