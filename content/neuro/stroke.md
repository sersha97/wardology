:::bottom
- **Last known well (LKW)** is the clock. Call the **stroke code**, check **glucose**, get **non-contrast CT + CTA**.
- **Disabling deficit ≤4.5 h, no contraindication → IV tenecteplase 0.25 mg/kg (max 25 mg)** or alteplase 0.9 mg/kg. 2026: both first-line.
- **Large-vessel occlusion → thrombectomy up to 24 h** — plan lysis and EVT **in parallel**.
- **BP:** <185/110 before lysis, **<180/105 for 24 h after**. No lysis → permissive up to 220/120.
- **Minor stroke (NIHSS ≤3) or high-risk TIA → DAPT (aspirin + clopidogrel) × 21 days**, then single agent.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Artery blocked → **core** (dead) + **penumbra** (stunned, salvageable). Penumbra dies by the minute: ~1.9 million neurons/min.
- Causes (TOAST): **large-artery atherosclerosis**, **cardioembolic** (AF!), **small vessel (lacunar)**, other (dissection, vasculitis, hypercoagulable), cryptogenic/ESUS.
- **TIA** = transient symptoms **without** infarct on MRI. High early stroke risk (most in 48 h).

## 🔎 Diagnosis

- **NIHSS** (0–42). Disabling = would stop the patient from basic activities or work (aphasia, hemianopia, weakness), even if NIHSS low.
- **Imaging:** non-contrast CT (bleed?) → **CTA head/neck** (LVO?) → CT perfusion/MRI for late windows.
- Mimics: hypoglycemia, seizure (Todd's), migraine, sepsis/toxic, conversion, old stroke "unmasked."
- **ABCD²** for TIA: Age ≥60 (1), BP ≥140/90 (1), Clinical: weakness (2) / speech only (1), Duration ≥60 min (2) / 10–59 (1), Diabetes (1). **≥4 = high risk**.

| Major lysis contraindications (check full list) |
|---|
| Intracranial hemorrhage on CT · ischemic stroke/head trauma <3 months · intracranial surgery <3 months · GI bleed <21 days · platelets <100 k · INR >1.7 · therapeutic LMWH <24 h · DOAC within 48 h (unless normal specific level/reversal) · BP not controlled · glucose <50 (treat and reassess) |

## 💊 Management

:::tabs
=== Hyperacute
- Tenecteplase **0.25 mg/kg single bolus (max 25 mg)** — not 0.4 mg/kg (harm).
- Or alteplase **0.9 mg/kg** (max 90 mg): 10% bolus, rest over 60 min.
- BP before lysis: labetalol 10–20 mg IV, nicardipine 5 mg/h titrate, clevidipine.
- **No antithrombotics for 24 h** after lysis; repeat CT at 24 h.
- Angioedema (ACEi users) → stop infusion, methylprednisolone, diphenhydramine, famotidine; airway.
- Symptomatic ICH after lysis → stop, cryoprecipitate 10 U, TXA, neurosurgery.
=== After the code (admission)
- **Swallow screen** before anything by mouth.
- Glucose **140–180**. Treat fever. Head of bed by aspiration risk.
- **DVT prophylaxis:** intermittent pneumatic compression (CLOTS-3) ± LMWH (after 24 h if lysed).
- **Workup:** MRI, vessel imaging, **TTE** (± bubble), **telemetry → long-term monitor** if cryptogenic, A1c, lipids.
- PT/OT/speech; rehab planning.
=== Secondary prevention
| Situation | Therapy |
|---|---|
| Minor stroke / high-risk TIA | **Aspirin + clopidogrel 21 days** (load clopidogrel 300–600 mg) |
| Other non-cardioembolic | Aspirin or clopidogrel |
| **AF** | **DOAC**; start early: within 48 h for minor/moderate, ~day 6–7 for major (ELAN, OPTIMAS) |
| Symptomatic carotid stenosis 70–99% | **CEA within 2 weeks** (stenting selected) |
| All | **High-intensity statin, LDL <70** (TST) · BP <130/80 · smoking cessation · diabetes control |
| PFO + young cryptogenic | PFO closure (selected) |
=== Hemorrhagic stroke (ICH)
- **SBP target ~140** (keep 130–150; avoid big drops).
- **Reverse anticoagulation:** warfarin → 4F-PCC + vit K · factor Xa → 4F-PCC (andexanet withdrawn from US, Dec 2025) · dabigatran → idarucizumab.
- No platelet transfusion for antiplatelet ICH (PATCH) unless surgery.
- Neurosurgery for cerebellar ICH >3 cm, hydrocephalus.
:::

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- Waiting for labs (except glucose) or MRI before tPA/TNK.
- Treating BP to "normal" in the first days without lysis → infarct growth.
- Letting a patient eat before swallow screen → aspiration pneumonia.
- Heparin drips for acute stroke (no benefit, more bleeding).

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| Aphasia, LKW 2 h ago, BP 200/110 | Lower BP <185/110 → thrombolysis |
| NIHSS 2, non-disabling, LKW 1 h | Antiplatelet (DAPT), not lysis |
| M1 occlusion, LKW 14 h, good perfusion mismatch | Thrombectomy |
| TIA, 80% ipsilateral carotid stenosis | CEA within 2 weeks |
| Stroke + new AF, small infarct | Start DOAC within ~48 h |
