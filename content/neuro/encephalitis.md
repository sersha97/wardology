:::bottom
- **Encephalitis = brain inflammation** → **altered mental status** (confusion, personality change, psychosis) ± fever, **seizures**, focal signs. Meningitis = stiff neck, mental status usually preserved early.
- Suspect it → **acyclovir 10 mg/kg IV q8h now**. HSV untreated kills ~70%.
- **MRI brain with contrast** (temporal lobes), **EEG** (non-convulsive seizures), **LP** with HSV-1/2 PCR.
- **Hydrate** and watch creatinine — acyclovir crystals cause **AKI**. Dose on **ideal body weight** in obesity.
- First HSV PCR negative in the first 72 h but picture fits → **repeat LP in 3–7 days** before stopping acyclovir.
- No infection found → **autoimmune encephalitis** (anti-NMDAR, LGI1): send serum + CSF panels, start **steroids/IVIG/PLEX** early, **search for a tumor**.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- **HSV-1** (~90% of adult HSV encephalitis) reactivates from the trigeminal ganglion and travels into the **temporal and frontal lobes** → **hemorrhagic necrosis**. Behavior change, aphasia, smell/taste hallucinations, seizures.
- **Autoimmune:** antibodies attack **surface brain receptors** (NMDA, LGI1, GABA-B, AMPA). Often triggered by a **tumor** (ovarian teratoma) or **after HSV encephalitis** (~25% develop anti-NMDAR).
- Other infections: **VZV** (elderly, immunosuppressed, with or without rash), **enteroviruses**, **arboviruses** (West Nile — summer, flaccid paralysis), CMV/HHV-6 (transplant), syphilis, TB, *Listeria* (rhombencephalitis).

## 🔎 Diagnosis

### Who to suspect

- Fever + **new confusion or behavior change** (+/- seizures) with no clear metabolic or toxic cause.
- **New psychiatric symptoms** with seizures, abnormal movements, autonomic swings → anti-NMDAR.
- **Faciobrachial dystonic seizures** (brief arm-face jerks, many per day) + **low Na** → **LGI1**.
- Rapid memory loss over weeks + seizures + MRI medial temporal lobes → **limbic encephalitis**.

### Tests

| Test | HSV encephalitis | Autoimmune |
|---|---|---|
| **CSF** | Lymphocytes 10–500, **RBCs** (hemorrhagic), protein ↑, glucose normal; **HSV PCR** (~96% sens, may be negative early) | Mild lymphocytes or normal; **oligoclonal bands**; antibodies in **CSF** |
| **MRI** | **Temporal lobe** T2/FLAIR (often one side), restricted diffusion, hemorrhage | Medial temporal (limbic) or normal (anti-NMDAR often normal) |
| **EEG** | **Periodic lateralized discharges (LPDs)** temporal | **Extreme delta brush** (anti-NMDAR); seizures |
| Other | — | **Pelvic MRI/US** (ovarian teratoma), CT chest/abdomen/pelvis, testicular US, PET |

:::tabs
=== Autoimmune encephalitis quick map
| Antibody | Picture | Tumor |
|---|---|---|
| **Anti-NMDAR** | Young woman: psychosis → seizures → **orofacial dyskinesias** → low consciousness, **autonomic instability**, hypoventilation | **Ovarian teratoma** (~40% of young women) |
| **LGI1** | Older man: memory loss, **faciobrachial dystonic seizures**, **hyponatremia** | Rare |
| **CASPR2** | Neuromyotonia, insomnia, pain (Morvan) | Thymoma |
| **GABA-B** | Severe seizures, limbic | **Small cell lung cancer** |
| **Anti-Hu / Ma2** (intracellular, paraneoplastic) | Limbic/brainstem, poor response | SCLC / testicular |
=== Mimics
- **Non-convulsive status** (get EEG), **septic/metabolic encephalopathy**, drug toxicity (cefepime, lithium, serotonin syndrome, NMS), **Wernicke**, PRES, stroke, CNS vasculitis, lymphoma, primary psychiatric illness (diagnosis of exclusion), **CJD** (rapid dementia + myoclonus).
:::

## 💊 Management

:::flow
Q: Possible encephalitis (fever + AMS, seizure, focal sign, CSF pleocytosis)?
Y: **Acyclovir 10 mg/kg IV q8h now** (+ meningitis coverage if bacterial not excluded). IV fluids.
Q: Seizures or fluctuating mental status?
Y: **EEG** (continuous if possible); treat seizures (levetiracetam). Watch for ICP rise.
Q: HSV PCR positive?
Y: Acyclovir **14–21 days** (21 in immunocompromised). Watch kidneys. Neuro-rehab.
N: Negative early but MRI/EEG fit → **repeat LP in 3–7 days**. Clear alternative diagnosis → stop acyclovir.
Q: Infections excluded, picture fits autoimmune?
Y: **Methylprednisolone 1 g/day × 5** ± **IVIG** or **PLEX** — do **not** wait for antibody results. **Remove tumor.** Second line: rituximab, cyclophosphamide.
:::

:::dose
- **Acyclovir:** 10 mg/kg IV q8h (ideal body weight; renal adjustment). Infuse over 1 h; give with fluids. Check creatinine daily.
- **Methylprednisolone:** 1 g IV daily × 5 days.
- **IVIG:** 0.4 g/kg/day × 5 days.
- **VZV encephalitis:** acyclovir 10–15 mg/kg q8h, 10–14 days.
:::

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- Waiting for the **MRI or LP** before acyclovir.
- Stopping acyclovir after **one early negative** PCR when the story fits.
- **Acyclovir AKI** (crystal nephropathy) and acyclovir **neurotoxicity** in renal failure — it can look like worsening encephalitis.
- Calling a young woman with **new psychosis + seizures** "primary psychiatric" → anti-NMDAR missed; haloperidol may then trigger NMS-like reactions.
- Missing **non-convulsive status** — always get EEG in unexplained AMS.
- Missing post-HSV **anti-NMDAR** relapse weeks later (new movements, psychosis) and calling it HSV relapse.

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| Fever, confusion, strange smells, MRI right temporal FLAIR, CSF RBCs | HSV encephalitis → **IV acyclovir** |
| Best next step in suspected HSV encephalitis (CT normal) | Start acyclovir (do not wait for PCR) |
| EEG in HSV encephalitis | Temporal periodic lateralized discharges |
| Young woman, psychosis, then seizures, orofacial dyskinesias, autonomic swings | **Anti-NMDAR** → look for **ovarian teratoma** |
| Older man, memory loss, brief arm-face jerks, Na 128 | **LGI1** limbic encephalitis |
