:::bottom
- **RLS** = **U**rge to move, **R**est makes it worse, **G**etting up relieves it, **E**vening/night (URGE). Look for **iron deficiency**, CKD, pregnancy, drugs (antihistamines, antidopaminergics, SSRIs).
- **Check ferritin and TSAT**: if **ferritin ≤75 ng/mL or TSAT <20%** → **iron** (oral or IV ferric carboxymaltose).
- Drugs: **gabapentin, gabapentin enacarbil, pregabalin** first. **Dopamine agonists** (pramipexole, ropinirole) → **augmentation** (earlier, worse, spreading symptoms); AASM 2024 suggests **against** their standard use.
- **REM sleep behavior disorder** (acting out dreams) → strong predictor of Parkinson disease/DLB.
- **Insomnia** → **CBT-I first**; avoid benzodiazepines and "Z-drugs" in older adults.
:::

## 😴 Narcolepsy

- Excessive daytime sleepiness ± **cataplexy** (sudden muscle weakness with laughter), hypnagogic hallucinations, sleep paralysis.
- **Polysomnography then MSLT**: mean sleep latency ≤8 min with ≥2 sleep-onset REM periods; type 1: low CSF hypocretin.
- Treat with modafinil/armodafinil, pitolisant, solriamfetol, sodium oxybate (cataplexy).

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- RLS augmentation management: check iron, then **switch to a gabapentinoid** (cross-taper) — don't keep increasing the agonist.
- RLS in pregnancy usually resolves after delivery; iron is the first step.
:::
