:::bottom
- **Nephritic**: hematuria, **red cell casts**, hypertension, rising creatinine. **Nephrotic**: proteinuria >3.5 g/d, low albumin, edema, lipids, bland sediment.
- **Low complement**: post-infectious, **lupus**, MPGN/cryoglobulinemia (HCV). **Normal**: IgA, ANCA, anti-GBM.
- **Anti-PLA₂R+** = primary membranous (highest VTE risk).
- Rapidly progressive GN → **biopsy urgently**, treat early (steroids + rituximab/cyclophosphamide; plasma exchange for anti-GBM).
:::

## 🔎 Nephritic causes

| Disease | Clue | Complement |
|---|---|---|
| **IgA nephropathy** | Hematuria **1–3 days** after URI | Normal |
| Post-streptococcal | **1–3 weeks** after pharyngitis | **Low C3** |
| Lupus nephritis | SLE, anti-dsDNA | Low C3/C4 |
| **ANCA vasculitis** | Sinus/lung disease (GPA), pauci-immune | Normal |
| **Anti-GBM** | Lung hemorrhage + GN, linear IgG | Normal |
| Cryoglobulinemic | HCV, purpura | Low C4 |

## 🔎 Nephrotic causes

| Disease | Clue |
|---|---|
| Minimal change | Children; adults with **NSAIDs, Hodgkin** |
| **FSGS** | Black patients (APOL1), obesity, **HIV**, heroin |
| **Membranous** | White adults; **anti-PLA₂R**; secondary — tumors, HBV, SLE V, NSAIDs |
| **Diabetic nephropathy** | Most common overall |
| Amyloidosis | Myeloma (AL), chronic inflammation (AA); big kidneys |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Nephrotic syndrome → **VTE risk** (renal vein thrombosis), infections, hyperlipidemia.
- Every nephrotic adult: SPEP/free light chains, HBV/HCV/HIV, ANA, PLA₂R.
:::
