:::bottom
- Evaluate with history, **IPSS**, DRE, **urinalysis**; PSA if it would change management.
- Bothersome LUTS → **α₁-blocker** (works in days).
- Prostate **>30–40 g** → add a **5α-reductase inhibitor** (works over months; lowers retention and surgery risk).
- **Surgery** for refractory symptoms or complications: retention, recurrent UTI, stones, hematuria, hydronephrosis.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Two components: **static** (gland size, driven by dihydrotestosterone) and **dynamic** (α₁-mediated smooth-muscle tone).
- Chronic obstruction thickens the bladder wall → urgency and frequency (storage symptoms) on top of weak stream (voiding symptoms).

## 💊 Management

| Drug | Notes |
|---|---|
| Tamsulosin, alfuzosin, silodosin | Less orthostasis than doxazosin/terazosin; floppy iris syndrome |
| Finasteride, dutasteride | Lowers PSA by ~50% (double the value for screening); sexual side effects |
| Tadalafil | When erectile dysfunction coexists |
| Mirabegron / antimuscarinic | Persistent storage symptoms with low post-void residual |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Anticholinergics, antihistamines and decongestants can precipitate **acute urinary retention**.
- Hematuria or a nodular prostate → evaluate for cancer, not BPH.
- Elevated creatinine with BPH → **renal ultrasound** for hydronephrosis.
:::
