**Effective date:** November 27, 2026 · **Version:** 2026-11-27

Wardology (wardology.com and the Wardology QBank) is operated by Serhii Shabalin, MD ("Wardology," "we," "us"). This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. **The short version:** we collect only what the QBank needs to work, we do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 📋 What we collect

**1. Information you give us when you register**

- Your **name** (optional) and **email address**.
- Your **password** — stored only as a salted, one-way cryptographic hash. We cannot see or recover your password.
- The date and version of the **Terms of Use and Privacy Policy you accepted**.

**2. Your QBank activity**

- Tests you create, their settings (mode, subjects, number of questions), and whether they are active, suspended or finished.
- For each question: the answer you chose, any answer you changed during an exam block, whether you were correct, questions you marked, and the time you spent.
- Statistics calculated from this activity (scores, usage, percentile rank).
- **Feedback** you send about a question.

**3. Information stored in your own browser**
Your browser keeps a few settings on your device, not on our servers: day/night theme, text size, your last Create-test choices, highlights you made in question stems, and crossed-out answer choices. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

**4. Technical information**

- A **session cookie** (`qb_s`) that keeps you logged in. It is strictly necessary, contains a random token only, and expires after 60 days or when you log out.
- Like every website, our hosting provider receives standard technical data with each request — such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested — to deliver the site, protect it from attacks and produce aggregate traffic statistics.

**What we do not collect:** payment information (Wardology is free), phone numbers, precise location, or **patient information**. Please never enter identifiable patient information anywhere on Wardology.

## 🎯 How we use it

- To **run the QBank**: create your account, log you in, save your progress, grade answers and show your performance.
- To show **anonymous peer statistics** — for example "62% answered correctly" or your percentile rank. These are computed from all users' first attempts and never show anyone's name or email.
- To **improve content**: questions that many people miss or flag are reviewed and corrected. We may use aggregated, de-identified answer statistics for this.
- To **keep the service secure** and prevent abuse such as scraping or account sharing.
- To **respond** to your feedback or messages.
- To send **essential service messages** (for example about your account or important changes to these policies). We do not send marketing email unless you separately opt in.

## 🤝 Who we share it with

We **do not sell or rent** personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

We use a small number of service providers that process data on our behalf, only to run the site:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Cloudflare, Inc.** | Website hosting, security and the QBank database (Cloudflare Pages, Workers and D1, US region) |
| **GitHub, Inc.** | Stores the site's source code and question content (no user accounts or answers) |
| **Google Fonts (Google LLC)** | Delivers the typefaces used on the site; your browser requests the fonts from Google, which receives your IP address |

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the safety of users or the public, to enforce our Terms, or as part of a transfer of Wardology to a new operator (who would remain bound by this policy).

## 🍪 Cookies

We use **one strictly necessary cookie** (`qb_s`) to keep you signed in to the QBank. We do not use advertising cookies, social-media pixels or cross-site tracking. Because the cookie is essential, the QBank cannot work without it.

## 🛡️ How we protect it

- All traffic is encrypted with **HTTPS**.
- Passwords are hashed with **PBKDF2-SHA-256** and a unique salt; session tokens are random and stored server-side.
- Answer keys and other users' data are never sent to your browser.
- Access to the database is limited to the site's operator.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you as required by law.

## 🗓️ How long we keep it

- **Account and QBank activity:** while your account exists. If you delete your account, your name, email, password hash, tests, answers and marks are deleted immediately.
- **Peer statistics:** the anonymous per-question answer counts (for example "34 people chose B") stay after deletion, because they contain no personal information.
- **Feedback:** kept to fix content; deleted with your account.
- **Server logs** at our hosting provider are kept for a short period according to the provider's own retention.
- Inactive accounts may be deleted after **3 years** without login.

## ✋ Your rights and choices

You can, at any time:

- **See** your activity in the QBank (Previous tests, Performance, Reports).
- **Reset** your QBank progress from **My account**.
- **Delete your account** and its data from **My account**.
- **Change your password** from **My account**.
- Email **hello@wardology.com** to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion, or to ask any privacy question.

Depending on where you live (for example California, other US states with privacy laws, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), you may have additional rights — such as access, correction, deletion, portability, objection or restriction — and the right to complain to your data-protection authority. We honor these requests wherever they apply and will not treat you differently for using them. We may need to confirm your identity first. For users in the EEA/UK, our legal bases are performing our agreement with you (running your account), our legitimate interests (security and improving content), and your consent where required.

## 🌍 International users

Wardology is operated from the United States, and data is stored in the United States. By using the Services from another country, you understand that your information will be processed in the US, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.

## 👶 Age

Wardology is intended for adults in medical education and practice. QBank accounts are for people **18 and older**. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13; if we learn that we have, we will delete it.

## 🔄 Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. For important changes, QBank users will be asked to review and accept the update at their next login.
