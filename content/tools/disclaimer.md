Wardology is for **education** of physicians and trainees. It is **not medical advice** and does not replace clinical judgment, local protocols, pharmacy review or specialist input.

- Doses are **typical adult doses** with normal kidney and liver function. Always check doses for renal/hepatic impairment, weight, age, pregnancy and interactions.
- Guidelines change. Every page shows a **last-reviewed date** and its sources. Pages are reviewed each quarter.
- ECG strips and diagrams are **schematic drawings** for teaching, not real patient tracings.
- No patient data is stored on this site.
