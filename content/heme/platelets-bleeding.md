:::bottom
- **Schistocytes + low platelets + normal PT/aPTT** → **TTP** until proven otherwise → **plasma exchange + steroids + caplacizumab** now.
- **Isolated thrombocytopenia, big platelets** → ITP → treat if <30k or bleeding (steroids ± IVIG).
- **Schistocytes + low platelets + high PT/aPTT + low fibrinogen** → **DIC** → treat the cause.
- **Mucosal bleeding since childhood** → VWF antigen, activity, factor VIII.
:::

## 🔎 Thrombocytopenia map

| Disorder | Smear | Coags | Key test/treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| ITP | Large platelets | Normal | Steroids, IVIG, TPO-RA |
| **TTP** | **Schistocytes** | Normal | ADAMTS13 <10%; plasma exchange |
| HUS (STEC) | Schistocytes | Normal | Bloody diarrhea, AKI; supportive |
| Atypical HUS | Schistocytes | Normal | Complement → eculizumab |
| **DIC** | Schistocytes | **Prolonged**, low fibrinogen | Treat cause |
| HIT | Normal | Normal | 4Ts; non-heparin anticoagulant |
| Pseudothrombocytopenia | **Clumps** | Normal | Redraw in citrate |

## 🧠 Platelet vs factor bleeding

- **Platelet/VWF**: mucosal, petechiae, menorrhagia, immediate bleeding.
- **Factor (hemophilia)**: joints, muscles, delayed bleeding.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Do **not** transfuse platelets in TTP or HIT unless life-threatening bleeding.
- Check **HIV and HCV** in all new adult ITP.
- Desmopressin works for type 1 VWD and mild hemophilia A.
:::
