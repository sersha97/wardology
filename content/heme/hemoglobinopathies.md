:::bottom
- **Acute chest syndrome**: new infiltrate + fever/chest pain/hypoxemia → O₂, **ceftriaxone + azithromycin**, incentive spirometry, **transfusion** (exchange if severe).
- Pain crisis: **rapid opioids** (within 60 minutes), cautious fluids.
- **Hydroxyurea** for frequent pain or prior ACS — reduces both.
- Microcytosis + **high RBC count, normal RDW, normal ferritin** → thalassemia trait → **Hb electrophoresis**; stop iron.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- **HbS** polymerizes when deoxygenated → rigid sickled cells → **vaso-occlusion** and **hemolysis**. Dehydration, cold, hypoxia and acidosis promote sickling.
- **Thalassemia** = too little α or β chain → small, fragile cells; marrow expands.

## 🔎 Sickle cell emergencies

| Emergency | Clue | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Acute chest syndrome | Infiltrate, hypoxemia | Antibiotics, transfusion |
| **Stroke** | Focal deficit | **Exchange transfusion** |
| Splenic sequestration | Falling Hb, big spleen (children) | Transfusion |
| Aplastic crisis | **Low reticulocytes**, parvovirus B19 | Transfusion |
| Priapism | >4 hours | Urology, aspiration |

## 🔎 Microcytosis

| | Iron deficiency | Thal trait | Anemia of inflammation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ferritin | ↓ | N/↑ | N/↑ |
| RDW | ↑ | N | N |
| RBC count | ↓ | **↑** | ↓ |
| Mentzer (MCV/RBC) | >13 | **<13** | >13 |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Transfuse in SCD to Hb ~10 g/dL max (hyperviscosity).
- β-thal trait → **HbA₂ >3.5%**; α-thal trait → normal electrophoresis.
:::
