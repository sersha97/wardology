:::bottom
- **MMR-deficient** CRC without BRAF/MLH1 methylation → **germline Lynch testing**.
- **Stage III colon cancer** → FOLFOX/CAPOX.
- **Aromatase inhibitor** → baseline **DXA**. **Tamoxifen** → VTE, endometrial cancer.
- **Checkpoint inhibitor toxicity** grade ≥2–3 → hold drug, **steroids**; colitis refractory → infliximab/vedolizumab.
- Anthracycline = cumulative, irreversible; **trastuzumab = reversible** cardiotoxicity.
:::

## 🔎 Paraneoplastic syndromes

| Syndrome | Tumor |
|---|---|
| **SIADH**, **Cushing (ectopic ACTH)**, **Lambert-Eaton**, anti-Hu | **Small cell lung** |
| Hypercalcemia (PTHrP) | **Squamous** (lung, head & neck), kidney, bladder |
| Dermatomyositis | Ovary, lung, GI, lymphoma |
| Trousseau (migratory thrombophlebitis) | Pancreas, adenocarcinomas |
| Erythrocytosis | Renal cell, hepatoma, cerebellar hemangioblastoma |
| Anti-NMDA encephalitis | Ovarian teratoma |

## 🔎 Immune-related adverse events

| Organ | Clue | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Colitis | Diarrhea | Most with **CTLA-4** |
| Endocrine | Fatigue, hyponatremia | **Hypophysitis**, thyroiditis, **type 1 DM** (no steroids help) |
| Pneumonitis | Cough, ground glass | Steroids |
| Hepatitis | High ALT | Steroids; mycophenolate if refractory |
| **Myocarditis** | Troponin ↑, often with myositis/MG | High mortality — high-dose steroids |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Surveillance after curative early breast cancer: history, examination, **annual mammogram** — no tumor markers or routine scans.
- 5-FU/capecitabine → coronary vasospasm; cisplatin → nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity, hypomagnesemia; bleomycin → pulmonary fibrosis.
:::
