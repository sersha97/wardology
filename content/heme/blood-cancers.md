:::bottom
- **CRAB** (Ca, renal, anemia, bone) → SPEP + immunofixation + **serum free light chains**.
- **MGUS**: M protein <3 g/dL, <10% plasma cells, no CRAB → monitor (no treatment).
- **APL**: DIC + Auer rods → **ATRA immediately**, cryo for fibrinogen.
- **CLL** early & asymptomatic → **watch and wait**.
- Erythrocytosis + low EPO → **JAK2** → phlebotomy to Hct <45% + aspirin.
:::

## 🔎 Plasma cell disorders

| | MGUS | Smoldering myeloma | Myeloma |
|---|---|---|---|
| M protein | <3 g/dL | ≥3 g/dL | Any |
| Marrow plasma cells | <10% | 10–59% | ≥10% (or plasmacytoma) |
| CRAB / SLiM | None | None | **Present** |

**SLiM** = ≥**60%** plasma cells, light chain ratio ≥**100**, >1 focal **MRI** lesion.

## 🔎 Myeloproliferative neoplasms

| | Mutation | Clue |
|---|---|---|
| CML | **BCR-ABL1** t(9;22) | High WBC with basophilia → TKI (imatinib) |
| Polycythemia vera | **JAK2** | Aquagenic pruritus, thrombosis, Budd-Chiari |
| Essential thrombocythemia | JAK2, CALR, MPL | Platelets >450k, erythromelalgia |
| Myelofibrosis | JAK2, CALR, MPL | Teardrop cells, huge spleen, dry tap |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Urine dipstick misses light chains — use UPEP or serum FLC.
- APL differentiation syndrome (fever, infiltrates, edema) → dexamethasone.
- CLL complications: hypogammaglobulinemia (infections), AIHA, ITP, Richter transformation, skin cancers.
:::
