:::bottom
- **A1c goal ~<7%** for most; less strict (<8%) for frail or older patients with limited life expectancy.
- **ASCVD** → add a **GLP-1 RA or SGLT2i** with proven benefit, regardless of A1c.
- **Heart failure or albuminuric CKD** → **SGLT2 inhibitor** first.
- **Obesity** → GLP-1 RA or tirzepatide.
- Avoid **hypoglycemia** — sulfonylureas and insulin are the usual culprits.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Type 2 diabetes = **insulin resistance** (liver, muscle, fat) + slowly failing **β-cells**.
- **GLP-1 RAs** boost glucose-dependent insulin, slow gastric emptying and reduce appetite → weight loss and fewer atherosclerotic events.
- **SGLT2 inhibitors** make the kidney spill glucose and sodium → lower glomerular pressure, natriuresis → protect the **heart and kidneys**.

## 🔎 Diagnosis

- Any one, confirmed by repeat (unless unequivocal hyperglycemia with symptoms): **A1c ≥6.5%**, fasting glucose **≥126 mg/dL**, 2-hour OGTT **≥200 mg/dL**, or random glucose ≥200 mg/dL with symptoms.
- Prediabetes: A1c 5.7%–6.4% → lifestyle program; consider metformin.

## 💊 Management

| Need | Drug class |
|---|---|
| ASCVD | GLP-1 RA (semaglutide, dulaglutide, liraglutide) or SGLT2i |
| HFrEF or HFpEF | SGLT2i (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin) |
| CKD with albuminuria | SGLT2i + ACE inhibitor/ARB; add finerenone or GLP-1 RA if needed |
| Weight loss | Tirzepatide or semaglutide |
| Cost | Metformin, sulfonylurea, pioglitazone, human insulin |

- Annual checks: **urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio and eGFR**, dilated **eye exam**, **foot exam** (monofilament), lipids; statin for age 40–75.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Metformin: hold for eGFR <30; long-term use → **vitamin B₁₂ deficiency** (check periodically).
- SGLT2i: genital mycotic infections, **euglycemic DKA** (hold 3 days before surgery), volume depletion.
- GLP-1 RA: nausea, gallbladder disease; avoid with personal/family history of **medullary thyroid cancer or MEN2**.
- Pioglitazone: fluid retention — avoid in heart failure.
:::
