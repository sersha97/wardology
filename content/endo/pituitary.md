:::bottom
- High prolactin: exclude **pregnancy, hypothyroidism, CKD, drugs** (antipsychotics, metoclopramide) → MRI.
- **Prolactinoma (any size) → cabergoline** first; surgery only if resistant/intolerant.
- **Male hypogonadism** = symptoms + **two low fasting morning testosterones** → LH/FSH to separate primary from secondary.
- Testosterone therapy suppresses sperm — avoid if fertility is a goal.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Dopamine from the hypothalamus **inhibits** prolactin. Anything that blocks dopamine (drugs, stalk compression) raises prolactin.
- Prolactin suppresses GnRH → **hypogonadism** (amenorrhea, low libido, infertility, bone loss).

## 🔎 Prolactin traps

| Trap | What it looks like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Stalk effect** | Big nonfunctioning adenoma, prolactin <100–150 | Surgery, not cabergoline |
| **Hook effect** | Giant adenoma with falsely modest prolactin | Ask lab to **dilute** |
| **Macroprolactin** | High prolactin, no symptoms | PEG precipitation |

## 🔎 Hypogonadism

| LH/FSH | Type | Next |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | Primary (testis) | Karyotype if small testes (Klinefelter) |
| Low/normal | Secondary (pituitary/hypothalamus) | Prolactin, iron studies, other pituitary hormones; MRI if T <150 ng/dL or mass symptoms |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- **Pituitary incidentaloma** → test for hypersecretion (prolactin, IGF-1, cortisol screen) and hypopituitarism; visual fields if near the chiasm.
- **Pituitary apoplexy** (sudden headache, visual loss, ophthalmoplegia) → **stress-dose hydrocortisone** first.
- Acromegaly: screen with **IGF-1**, confirm with failed GH suppression on OGTT.
:::
