:::bottom
- Confirm **Whipple triad**: symptoms + glucose <55 mg/dL + relief with glucose.
- Draw labs **during** the episode: glucose, insulin, **C-peptide, proinsulin**, β-hydroxybutyrate, **sulfonylurea screen**.
- High insulin + **low C-peptide** = **exogenous insulin**. High insulin + high C-peptide + **positive screen** = sulfonylurea. Negative screen = **insulinoma**.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- β cells cleave proinsulin into **insulin + C-peptide** (1:1). Pharmaceutical insulin has no C-peptide.
- Insulin suppresses ketone production → **low β-hydroxybutyrate** in insulin-mediated hypoglycemia.

## 🔎 Pattern table

| Cause | Insulin | C-peptide | Proinsulin | SU screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Exogenous insulin | ↑ | ↓ | ↓ | − |
| Sulfonylurea | ↑ | ↑ | ↑ | + |
| Insulinoma | ↑ | ↑ | ↑ | − |
| Non-insulin (adrenal insufficiency, IGF-2 tumor, liver failure, sepsis, alcohol) | ↓ | ↓ | ↓ | − |

## 🧪 Making it happen

- Spontaneous episode not available? **72-hour supervised fast** (fasting hypoglycemia) or mixed-meal test (postprandial — e.g., after gastric bypass).
- Insulinoma localization: CT/MRI, endoscopic ultrasound, GLP-1 receptor PET.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Health care workers and relatives of patients with diabetes → think **factitious**.
- Postprandial hypoglycemia after Roux-en-Y gastric bypass → dietary change, acarbose.
:::
