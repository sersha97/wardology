:::bottom
- Screen everyone **15–65** at least once; PrEP for anyone at substantial risk.
- **Acute HIV**: fever, rash, pharyngitis, **mucocutaneous ulcers** after exposure → **4th-gen Ag/Ab + HIV RNA**.
- **Start ART rapidly** (ideally within days) — except **cryptococcal and TB meningitis**.
- **CD4 <200 → TMP-SMX** (PJP); <100 + Toxo IgG+ → TMP-SMX covers it.
:::

## 💊 Prevention

| Strategy | Use |
|---|---|
| **PrEP** — F/TDF daily, F/TAF daily (not receptive vaginal), cabotegravir q2mo, lenacapavir q6mo | Ongoing risk |
| **PEP** — 3-drug ART × 28 days within **72 h** | Single exposure |
| **U = U** | Undetectable viral load → no sexual transmission |

## 🔎 Opportunistic infections by CD4

| CD4 | Infections |
|---|---|
| <200 | **PJP**, thrush, esophageal candidiasis |
| <100 | **Toxoplasma**, **cryptococcus** |
| <50 | **CMV retinitis**, **MAC** |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- PJP: hypoxemia, LDH ↑, bilateral ground glass; **prednisone if Pao₂ <70** or A–a >35.
- IRIS after ART start: paradoxical worsening of an OI — continue ART, treat inflammation.
- Abacavir → test **HLA-B*57:01** first.
:::
