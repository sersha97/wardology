:::bottom
- **Erythema migrans** → **doxycycline 10 days**, no serology.
- High-degree AV block from Lyme → IV ceftriaxone + temporary pacing.
- Fever after travel to a malaria area → **thick and thin smears ×3** (+ rapid test). Severe → **IV artesunate**.
- Suspected **RMSF or ehrlichiosis** → **doxycycline immediately** (any age), don't wait for tests.
:::

## 🔎 Tick-borne diseases

| Disease | Tick / region | Clue | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lyme** | *Ixodes*, Northeast/Upper Midwest | EM rash, facial palsy, AV block, knee arthritis | Doxycycline |
| **Anaplasmosis / ehrlichiosis** | Ixodes / Lone Star | Fever, **leukopenia, thrombocytopenia, ↑ AST/ALT**, morulae | Doxycycline |
| **Babesiosis** | Ixodes, Northeast | Hemolysis, **"Maltese cross"** on smear; asplenia → severe | Atovaquone + azithromycin |
| **RMSF** | Dermacentor, Southeast/Southwest | Rash wrists/ankles → palms/soles, hyponatremia | **Doxycycline** now |

## 🔎 Fever after travel — by incubation

| Incubation | Think |
|---|---|
| <14 days | Dengue (retro-orbital pain, low platelets), chikungunya, rickettsia, leptospirosis, **malaria** |
| 1–3 weeks | **Malaria** (P. falciparum), **typhoid** (relative bradycardia, rose spots) |
| >3 weeks | Malaria (vivax/ovale), TB, hepatitis, schistosomiasis |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Single-dose doxycycline 200 mg within 72 h of removing an engorged Ixodes tick in an endemic area.
- P. vivax/ovale → add **primaquine/tafenoquine** (check G6PD first) for liver hypnozoites.
:::
