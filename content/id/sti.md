:::bottom
- **Gonorrhea → ceftriaxone 500 mg IM** once (1 g if ≥150 kg).
- **Chlamydia → doxycycline 100 mg BID × 7 days** (azithromycin in pregnancy).
- **Syphilis**: early (<1 year) → benzathine penicillin 2.4 MU **once**; late/unknown → **weekly × 3**; neuro/ocular/otic → **IV penicillin 10–14 days**.
- Always test for HIV; treat partners; retest in 3 months.
:::

## 🔎 Syphilis stages

| Stage | Findings |
|---|---|
| Primary | Painless chancre |
| Secondary | Rash incl. palms/soles, condylomata lata, mucous patches, lymphadenopathy |
| Latent | Serology only — early (<1 y) vs late/unknown |
| Tertiary | Gummas, aortitis, tabes dorsalis |
| Neurosyphilis | Any stage — meningitis, cranial neuropathy, ocular, otic |

## 🧠 Serology in 60 seconds

- **Nontreponemal** (RPR, VDRL): titers follow activity; **fourfold drop** = response.
- **Treponemal** (FTA-ABS, TP-PA, EIA): stays positive for life.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- **Penicillin allergy in pregnancy or neurosyphilis** → desensitize, then penicillin.
- **Doxy-PEP** (200 mg within 72 h of sex) reduces bacterial STIs in MSM and transgender women with a recent STI.
- Disseminated gonococcal infection: tenosynovitis, dermatitis, polyarthralgia → ceftriaxone 1 g IV daily.
:::
