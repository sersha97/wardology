:::bottom
- **Pneumococcal**: all adults **≥50** → one dose **PCV20 or PCV21** (or PCV15 → PPSV23).
- **Recombinant zoster**: all adults **≥50** (and ≥19 if immunocompromised) — 2 doses, even after shingles.
- **Hepatitis B**: all adults **19–59**; ≥60 with risk factors or on request.
- **Tdap** once, then Td or Tdap every 10 years; Tdap in **every pregnancy** (27–36 weeks).
- **Influenza** every year for everyone ≥6 months.
- **Live vaccines** (MMR, varicella) are **contraindicated** in pregnancy and significant immunosuppression.
:::

## 🧠 Why conjugates win

- Polysaccharide vaccines activate B cells without T-cell help → weaker, short-lived response, no memory.
- Conjugating the polysaccharide to a protein carrier recruits T cells → stronger response and **immunologic memory**.

## 💊 Schedule highlights

| Vaccine | Who |
|---|---|
| PCV20 / PCV21 | All ≥50; 19–49 with risk conditions (CKD, chronic heart/lung/liver disease, diabetes, smoking, alcohol use disorder, immunocompromise, CSF leak, cochlear implant) |
| Recombinant zoster | ≥50 (2 doses, 2–6 months apart) |
| RSV | All ≥75; 50–74 at increased risk (one dose) |
| HPV | Through age 26; shared decision 27–45 |
| Hepatitis B | 19–59 universally |
| MenACWY / MenB | Asplenia, complement deficiency, eculizumab, college dorms (MenACWY) |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- **Asplenia**: pneumococcal, meningococcal (ACWY + B) and *Haemophilus influenzae* type b — ideally ≥2 weeks before elective splenectomy.
- Egg allergy is **not** a contraindication to influenza vaccine.
- HIV with CD4 <200: avoid live vaccines (MMR, varicella).
- Give vaccines **≥2 weeks before** starting immunosuppressive biologics when possible.
:::
