:::bottom
- Positive IGRA/TST → **symptoms + chest radiograph** to exclude active TB.
- **Latent TB → rifamycin regimens**: **4 months rifampin** (4R), 3HP weekly, or 3HR.
- **Active TB → RIPE** (rifampin, isoniazid, pyrazinamide, ethambutol) × 2 months, then RI × 4 months; airborne isolation.
- Screen for TB before **TNF inhibitors, JAK inhibitors, transplant**.
:::

## 🔎 Tests

| Test | Pro | Con |
|---|---|---|
| **IGRA** | One visit; **not affected by BCG** | Cost |
| TST | Cheap | BCG false positives; needs return visit |
| Sputum AFB smear/culture + NAAT | Active disease | Not for LTBI |

TST positive cutoffs: **≥5 mm** (HIV, immunosuppressed, close contact, fibrotic CXR), **≥10 mm** (recent immigrants, IV drug use, health care workers, residents of congregate settings), **≥15 mm** (no risk factors).

## 💊 Drug toxicities

| Drug | Toxicity |
|---|---|
| Isoniazid | Hepatitis, **peripheral neuropathy** (give pyridoxine) |
| Rifampin | Orange fluids, **drug interactions**, hepatitis |
| Pyrazinamide | Hepatitis, **hyperuricemia** |
| Ethambutol | **Optic neuritis** (red-green color vision) |

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Rifampin induces CYP3A4: contraceptives, warfarin, DOACs, many antiretrovirals fail.
- Untreated LTBI risk: ~5–10% lifetime; ~10%/year with HIV.
:::
