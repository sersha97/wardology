:::bottom
- Ask everyone ≥65: **fallen in the past year? feel unsteady? worried about falling?**
- Test **gait, strength and balance** (Timed Up and Go ≥12 s = increased risk; 30-second chair stand).
- **Exercise** (balance + strength, tai chi, PT) is the best single intervention.
- **Deprescribe** sedatives, anticholinergics, antipsychotics and excess antihypertensives.
- **Vitamin D does not prevent falls** unless the patient is deficient.
:::

## 🧠 Why older adults fall

- Falls come from many small deficits adding up: weak legs, slow reflexes, poor vision, neuropathy, orthostatic hypotension, cognitive impairment, medications and home hazards.
- A fall can be the only sign of acute illness (infection, arrhythmia, anemia).

## 🔎 Assessment

- Orthostatic vital signs, vision, feet and footwear, neuropathy, cognition, continence, home safety.
- Medications: benzodiazepines, Z-drugs, opioids, anticholinergics, antipsychotics, antidepressants, α-blockers.
- Bone health — falls plus osteoporosis = fractures.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- **Hip protectors** reduce hip fractures in nursing homes only with good adherence; they do not prevent falls.
- Physical restraints **increase** injury.
- After a hip fracture, start osteoporosis therapy (zoledronic acid improves survival after hip fracture).
:::
