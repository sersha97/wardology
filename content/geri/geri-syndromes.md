:::bottom
- **Dementia work-up**: B₁₂, TSH, **one brain image**, screen for depression, hearing/vision, offending drugs.
- **Incontinence**: urge → bladder training; stress → pelvic floor training; high PVR → overflow. Avoid anticholinergics in older adults (use mirabegron).
- **Pressure injury**: stage by the **deepest visible tissue** (fat = 3, bone/tendon = 4).
- **Beers**: stop sulfonylureas (glyburide), first-generation antihistamines, benzodiazepines, sliding-scale insulin when possible.
:::

## 🧠 Dementia vs delirium vs depression

| | Dementia | Delirium | Depression |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onset | Months–years | **Hours–days** | Weeks |
| Attention | Preserved early | **Impaired** | Usually OK |
| Course | Progressive | Fluctuating | Episodic |

Cholinesterase inhibitors (donepezil, rivastigmine, galantamine) for mild-to-moderate Alzheimer disease; memantine for moderate-to-severe. Anti-amyloid antibodies (lecanemab, donanemab) for early AD with confirmed amyloid — watch for ARIA (APOE ε4 raises risk).

## 🔎 Pressure injury stages

| Stage | Deepest tissue |
|---|---|
| 1 | Intact skin, nonblanchable redness |
| 2 | Dermis (partial thickness, blister) |
| 3 | **Fat** (full thickness) |
| 4 | **Bone, tendon, muscle, fascia** |
| Unstageable | Base hidden by slough/eschar |
| Deep tissue injury | Purple/maroon intact skin |

## 💊 Beers highlights

- Glyburide, glimepiride, sliding-scale insulin → hypoglycemia.
- Diphenhydramine, hydroxyzine, oxybutynin, TCAs → anticholinergic (confusion, retention, falls).
- Benzodiazepines, Z-drugs → falls, fractures, delirium.
- Long-term PPIs without indication; NSAIDs with CKD or anticoagulants.

## 🎓 Board corner

:::board
- Feeding tubes in advanced dementia do **not** prolong life or prevent aspiration — hand feeding is recommended.
- Normal-pressure hydrocephalus: **wet, wobbly, wacky** → MRI, large-volume LP.
:::
